\title{A Candidate-Invariant Cost Volume in a Deployed Stereo Network: Separating Stereo Accuracy from Identifiable Geometric Correspondence}

\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Anonymous Authors}
\IEEEauthorblockA{Manuscript prepared from repository records only.\\
No new experiment, measurement, or citation was produced for this paper.}}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Background.} Cost-volume stereo networks are widely assumed to perform a disparity search over candidate slices, an assumption rarely tested on deployed artefacts. \textbf{Gap.} The signatures normally taken as evidence of geometric correspondence have not been checked against models that provably cannot search. \textbf{Method.} We forensically reconstruct a deployed production stereo network (Hailo's StereoNet), train under a bit-reproducible deterministic harness with pre-registered interventions, and apply an adversarial protocol in which each candidate signature must survive an explicit correspondence-free counterexample. \textbf{Main empirical result.} The deployed model's cost volume is candidate-invariant by algebraic identity ($\max_k |V_k - V_0| = 0.0$); the model reaches 8.154\% D1 under the official protocol and loses approximately 89--90 D1 points under right-image ablation in the EXP-007 protocol (10 scenes, 1\,837\,304 px). A controlled $2\times2$ shows candidate-varying content and readout conditioning interact (2.2955 vs.\ 8.3717 EPE on the deterministic 40-scene pooled set; plain cells are H1-v2 10-scene late-window means). The frozen volume still consumes $L_f - R_f$ at a single alignment, so this is a statement about the absence of a disparity search, not about the absence of all geometric use. \textbf{Identification result.} Across eight intervention families, each apparently geometric signature admits a correspondence-free counterexample, and two failures are structural. Genuine local geometric correspondence is therefore \textbf{not demonstrated} by these experiments, which is not the same as showing it absent. \textbf{Significance.} Stereo performance and \emph{identifiable} geometric correspondence can come apart, and mechanism claims in cost-volume stereo need stronger evidence than the standard signatures. \textbf{Limitation.} One architecture, one dataset, 160-scene from-scratch training, three seeds, no significance tests.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Can stereo accuracy and geometric correspondence be experimentally separated in a cost-volume stereo network, and what can controlled interventions establish about which of the two a given network has? This question presupposes nothing: a negative answer---that accuracy and correspondence always co-occur and interventions attribute cleanly---is a coherent outcome the evidence could have produced and did not.

This paper reports two findings. First, the deployed model retains useful stereo prediction despite a candidate-invariant cost volume. All twelve candidate slices are the same tensor; this is an algebraic identity of the construction, not an empirical observation. The network still consumes a single-alignment binocular difference, so this is a statement about the absence of a disparity search, not about the absence of all geometric use. Second, each of the standard signatures used to attribute correspondence admits an explicit correspondence-free counterexample, and two of the failures are structural rather than incidental. Genuine local geometric correspondence is \textbf{not demonstrated} by these experiments; this is not the same as showing it absent.

The paper is organised as follows. Section~\ref{sec:model} defines the deployed model and the two hypotheses under test. Section~\ref{sec:methods} documents the determinism, pre-registration, and protocol discipline that make the numbers comparable. Section~\ref{sec:pillar1} establishes the candidate-invariant volume with strong binocular dependence. Section~\ref{sec:pillar2} shows, in a controlled factorial, that candidate-varying cost-volume geometry contributes materially to the trained solution under this recipe. Section~\ref{sec:pillar3} presents the identification boundary as one adversarial-methodology argument. Section~\ref{sec:sensitivity} reports architectural sensitivity, and Sections~\ref{sec:discussion}--\ref{sec:conclusion} discuss, limit, and conclude.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Cost-volume stereo descends from the StereoNet design of Khamis \emph{et al.}~\cite{khamis2018stereonet}: a low-resolution matching volume, sub-pixel soft-argmin readout, and learned guided refinement. The deployed artefact studied here is Hailo's compiled StereoNet baseline~\cite{hailo2023stereonet}, whose published accuracy figure and benchmark context are documented in~\cite{hailo2023benchmark}; the upstream PyTorch source it derives from is the nivosco reimplementation~\cite{nivosco_stereonet}. Evaluation uses the KITTI 2015 stereo data and disparity ground truth~\cite{geiger2015kitti}.

The field has explored four broad answers to the cost of 3D aggregation: smaller volumes, more informative volumes (group-wise correlation, attention volumes), cheaper replacements for 3D convolution (sparse-point, channel excitation), and abandoning the volume (tile hypotheses, recurrent updates). \todo{CITATION NEEDED: competitor survey beyond abstracts --- group-wise correlation, attention concatenation volumes, iterative refinement methods; repo records SR-040--SR-050 were inspected at abstract level only and cannot support full citations.} No competitor was implemented or measured in this project, and no cross-paper accuracy comparison is attempted: the repo's competitive-landscape record establishes that published figures use mutually incomparable protocols.

Mechanistic interpretability of stereo networks---gradient flow, softmax sharpness, pruning sensitivity, oracle substitution as mechanism probes---is the second relevant literature. \todo{CITATION NEEDED: interpretability and identifiability literature for deep vision models.} Our contribution is to test each such proxy against an explicit correspondence-free counterexample in a system where the ground truth (candidate invariance) is known by construction.

\section{The Deployed Model and Problem Formulation}
\label{sec:model}

The deployed network takes rectified stereo pairs at $368\times1232$ and outputs full-resolution disparity. Feature extraction (shared Siamese weights, 199\,552 parameters per branch occurrence) downsamples $16\times$ to $23\times77$ with 32 channels. The cost volume is constructed per disparity candidate $k \in 0\ldots11$ as $V[k] = \mathrm{shift}_k(L_f) - R_f$, stacked to $1\times32\times12\times23\times77$, aggregated by five 3D convolutions (111\,585 parameters, 2.369~GMAC, 4.2\% of the model), upsampled to full resolution before a soft-argmin, and refined by six dilated residual blocks (dilations 1,2,4,8,1,1; 112\,449 parameters; 50.79~GMAC; 90.6\% of arithmetic). Unique parameters total 423\,586; total MACs 56.04~GMAC at $368\times1232$. The model description here is verified against the exported graph; the upstream source~\cite{nivosco_stereonet} and the Model Zoo configuration~\cite{hailo2023stereonet} are recorded as the provenance chain.

Two hypotheses organise the paper. $H_{\mathrm{CORR}}$: the network's stereo behaviour is produced by genuine geometric correspondence implemented as candidate search in the cost volume. $H_{\mathrm{ALT}}$: the behaviour is produced without candidate-varying correspondence information---for example through a single-alignment binocular difference consumed by downstream stages. ``Identifiable'' here has a fixed operational meaning: a signature counts as evidence for $H_{\mathrm{CORR}}$ only if no correspondence-free mechanism reproduces it. A test that fires on a provably search-free model is not evidence, however geometric it looks.

First, \textbf{determinism}: the historical harness was not bit-reproducible (same seed, epoch-0 loss 10.8798 vs.\ 10.5762; bilinear backward isolated as one nondeterministic operation). \section{A Candidate-Invariant Cost Volume with Strong Binocular Dependence}
\label{sec:pillar1}

The deployed cost volume contains no candidate-varying information. In the exported graph, each disparity shift is implemented as a pad-right followed by a slice from zero, so $\mathrm{shift}_k$ is the identity for every $k$; all twelve candidate slices are the same tensor, and $\max_k |V_k - V_0| = 0.0$ exactly over 5 measured scenes. The construction is present in the upstream PyTorch source, so it survives export and compilation rather than being an export artefact, and it is re-confirmed at 0.0 across 6\,160 permutation arms and 816 arms over 17 weight-sets including 16 random initialisations. Invariance is therefore a property of the volume construction, not of any particular weights. The network still consumes a single-alignment binocular difference $L_f - R_f$ inside that volume, so this is a statement about the absence of a disparity search, not about the absence of all geometric use.

The same model is nevertheless strongly binocular and usefully accurate. Under the official protocol the pretrained weights reach D1 8.154\% (EPE 1.3134~px; fp32 CUDA tabulation 1.314~px / 8.155\% / 90.1~ms recorded separately). Under the distinct EXP-007 ablation protocol (10 scenes, 1\,837\,304 px, baseline EPE 1.4422 / D1 9.3315), five right-image ablations each cost roughly +89 to +90 D1 points: right:=left +89.678, other-scene +89.957, horizontal flip +89.128, black +89.649, noise +89.806, with baseline prediction correlation falling to $-0.310$, $+0.00019$, $+0.121$, $+0.348$, and $-0.147$ respectively. A monocular predictor is by definition unaffected by any of these interventions. The deployed model retains useful stereo prediction despite a candidate-invariant cost volume, and useful binocular stereo behaviour can exist without candidate-varying correspondence information in the cost volume. The ablation deltas are computed strictly within the EXP-007 protocol and are never subtracted from the 8.154\% official-protocol figure.

\section{Shift $\times$ Readout: What Candidate-Varying Geometry Contributes}
\label{sec:pillar2}

Neither a genuinely shifted volume nor a conditioned readout alone produces a binocular trained model; only their combination does. The complete $2\times2$, run at matched recipe with 423\,586 parameters in every cell, reads: shift=none with plain readout 14.423~EPE, shift=left with plain readout 14.414~EPE (both H1-v2 10-scene late-window means, pre-determinism harness), shift=none with standardised readout 8.3717466~EPE / 66.5977700\% D1, shift=left with standardised readout 2.2955182~EPE / 15.6045143\% D1 (both deterministic 40-scene pooled). One changed variable per cell. The shift without the readout fix is worth $-0.008$~px; the readout fix without the shift leaves EPE at 8.37; the combination reaches 2.30. Candidate-varying cost-volume geometry contributes materially to the trained solution under this recipe. The 6.08~px gap is approximately $59\times$ the separately measured 6-block three-seed EPE range of 0.1027~px, which is the stated defence of the single-seed-per-cell design; the result is 160 scenes from random initialisation and is not a competitive-accuracy claim. Directional corroboration comes from H3, where removing candidate-varying content under the standardised readout returns right-image $\Delta$D1 of $-1.08\ldots+1.91$---the monocular signature---showing the ablation is capable of detecting monocularity when present.

A readout change alone makes a trained model binocular: standardising the aggregated cost before the frozen soft-argmin takes softmax entropy 0.0000 to 1.67--1.83~nats, matching-path gradient from 5/150 crops to 16\,000/16\,000 batches, $r(\mathrm{disparity\_initial},\mathrm{GT})$ from $-0.001\ldots+0.283$ to $+0.978\ldots+0.988$, right-image $\Delta$D1 from $\le+0.8$ to $+74.88\ldots+83.95$~pt, and EPE from 14.41 to 4.199/3.745/4.333 across three seeds. Binocular dependence is a property of the whole trained configuration: it is destroyed by truncating refinement below five blocks and by moving the readout temperature away from its trained value in either direction (Section~\ref{sec:sensitivity}). Explicitly, this section claims materiality, not mechanism: a candidate-pointwise latch reproduces the downstream candidate-axis signatures without correspondence, the statistic used is architecturally available without correspondence, and a search-free model still responds to translation---so the factorial measures information content, not how it is used. Correspondence-free use of candidate-varying content is the surviving alternative we do not adjudicate.

\section{Adversarial Identification Analysis}
\label{sec:pillar3}

A correspondence signature counts here only if no correspondence-free mechanism reproduces it. What follows is one adversarial-methodology argument---apparent signature, correspondence-free counterexample, stronger intervention, new counterexample or identifiability failure, boundary---not a chronology of experiments. Individual diagnostic records belong in the supplement and are referenced by ID.

Three counterexamples, strongest first. First, a provably search-free model still responds to translation: SHIFT-001 measures a maximum translation response of 0.6975 candidates, with $\mathrm{disparity\_initial}$ 3.6452--4.2717 against the pre-registered prediction of 5.5 (maximum deviation 1.8548). Translation response therefore cannot serve as correspondence evidence; the test fires on the wrong hypothesis, which is the live positive control proving the tests were capable of firing at all. Second, matching-path gradient is not a stereo proxy: EMERGENCE-001 finds gradient on 100\% of batches at every checkpoint, including seed~2 at epoch~20 where corrupting the right image \emph{improves} D1 by 0.91~pt, with re-measurement reproducing training-time ablation to 0.00~pt. Third, candidate-axis ordering is not discriminating: across 24\,640 INDEX-001/002 arms a random permutation reproduces the ordered pattern, with the algebraic null at 6\,160 arms all at 0.0. A candidate-pointwise latch reproduces the signature in a provably degenerate model (O1 audit, scored not-identifiable without execution).

The strongest attempted intervention failed structurally. Zone-local translation was designed to confine the statistic to a zone; instead ZI-1 and ZI-2 are both 0.0 bit-exact across 3 seeds and every $\Delta\in\{1,3,6\}$, with clean interiors $[0,224)$/$[912,1136)$: under a bounded receptive field the zone-local statistic \emph{is} the global one. This holds for any zone-local design, regardless of who designs it---it is architectural, not incidental.

The null-impossibility argument, in two lines: for a fixed left image, a null that is content-matched to the test pair inside the tested window necessarily shares the content whose geometric status is under test, so no paired null over fixed-$L$ interventions can be simultaneously content-matched and geometrically non-corresponding. Its concrete consequences are A4s (scene-identity conditioning) and A5r (readout pinning), which survive even the feasible artefact-free spatially varying field gate (D-y kills A3/A5/A9/M1; A4s/A5r survive because the obstruction is intervention-agnostic).

The boundary is Table~\ref{tab:t4} and Fig.~\ref{fig:f7}: eight intervention families, each closed by counterexample or structural result. What they share is that correspondence never varies independently of content in any available data, so every signature has a content-matched correspondence-free twin. Crossing the boundary requires data in which correspondence varies independently of content---multi-view or multi-baseline capture, or renderer control with its own conceded ceiling. We establish the boundary for the families examined and give the structural reason it is expected to extend; the fallback, stated here rather than defended against, is a boundary for translation-based and field-based interventions on fixed-left-image pairs.

Three hostile criticisms, answered where the blueprint marks them. First: the candidate-invariant volume only proves the aggregation cannot use candidate variation, since the network still receives $L-R$ information. Valid, entirely---it is the correct reading, and EXP-007's +89--90~pt dependence supports the reviewer. We concede and adopt it: the claim is stated as ``no candidate-varying correspondence information in the cost volume,'' never as ``no correspondence,'' in the title, abstract, every results paragraph, and the conclusion; the residual binocular channel might be geometric, which is why Section~\ref{sec:pillar3} exists. A casual citer may still compress this to ``StereoNet doesn't do stereo matching''; the disclaimer sits in the abstract, not only in Limitations, as mitigation. Second: 8.15\% D1 may reflect monocular prediction. No, as stated---five right-image ablations cost +89.128 to +89.957~pt (noise +89.806), correlation falls to $-0.310\ldots+0.348$, and H3 shows the ablation detects monocularity when present ($\Delta$D1 $-1.08\ldots+1.91$); the baseline is the 9.3315\% EXP-007 figure, not 8.154\%. Dependence is still not geometric use---that is the thesis, not a forced concession. Third: the intervention failures merely show poor experimental design. Partially, and the honest answer says so: GEOM-001's halt at HS1, the invalid epoch-10 gate recalibrated from a control, the diverging inverse-oracle that falsely confirmed with ratio 1.000, and Zone-001's $S$ adopted before its collapse---those are errors, recorded as errors in the supplement, and carry no boundary weight. That the boundary is not merely incompetence rests on four points: the counterexamples are constructions (latch, A4s, A5r, M1); ZI-2${}={}$0.0 is architectural; the null-impossibility argument references no intervention; and SHIFT-001 is a live positive control.

Four methodological negatives transfer beyond this system: gradient flow, softmax sharpness (T${}={}$0.25 gives entropy 0.290~nats yet right-dependence +0.57/$-2.98$/$-1.17), inference-time pruning sensitivity (dropping block~4 costs seed~0 +0.19~px but seeds~1/2 +24.16/+16.37~px---a one-seed study would call block~4 free), and oracle substitution (a diverging inverse-oracle returned ratio 1.000 and falsely confirmed the pre-registered verdict). Each is a direct measurement in this system, presented as a caution rather than a law.

\section{Architectural Sensitivity}
\label{sec:sensitivity}

Block depth, readout temperature, and refinement truncation were tested as empirical sensitivity, not causal attribution. The deterministic $3\times3$ (blocks $\times$ seeds, 200 epochs, same 40 scenes and 3\,802\,797 px) in Table~\ref{tab:t5} gives: five blocks carry a reproducible EPE penalty relative to six at three seeds---ranges disjoint by +0.0748~px, 9/9 pairings---with the D1 exception always quoted (ranges overlap by 0.13~pt; 5-block seed~1 beats 6-block seed~1). The four-block arm straddles the frozen boundary $[2.2955182, 2.3982217]$ (seed~0 inside, seeds~1--2 above), 7/9 pairings, D1 overlap 1.36~pt, own seed spread 0.3166~px ($3.08\times$ the reference arm's and larger than the 0.1603~px gap it would need to show): inconclusive, and no fourth seed should be bought. \section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Performance and identifiable correspondence come apart. The deployed network proves the first half by existence: useful stereo prediction without candidate-varying correspondence information in the cost volume, with the single-alignment $L_f - R_f$ channel load-bearing. The factorial proves the second half is non-trivial: candidate-varying geometry is material once the readout permits learning, so the deployed degeneracy cannot be dismissed as ``the volume never matters.'' The boundary then delimits what follows: none of the standard interventions decides whether the from-scratch binocular models---or the residual binocular channel of the deployed model---implement genuine geometric correspondence, because each signature has a content-matched correspondence-free twin and two obstructions are structural. Training installs a calibrated candidate-axis response that untrained aggregations do not produce (TR-001 median +1.0014, min +0.9534, 12/12 outside; random median +0.0032, max +0.2441; 0/384 qualify, 378/384 flat)---reported as a trained-vs-untrained separation, never read as matching. Error accounting (about one third upstream, two thirds downstream by the held-out fixed point: 29.3/41.5/32.9\% removed) and the late emergence of stereo dependence (onset after epoch~10--50, saturating after~150) are budgets and timelines, not mechanisms.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

One architecture; one dataset; 160-scene from-scratch training from random initialisation; $n=3$ seeds with no computable p-values; single-seed factorial cells defended only by the $59\times$ effect-to-seed-scale ratio; inference-only readout and pruning studies; the generalisation step in the boundary claim, argued beyond the enumerated families with the stated fallback; no literature accuracy comparator. \todo{CITATION NEEDED: literature anchor for what 8.154\% D1 means against contemporary stereo networks.} Genuine local geometric correspondence is not demonstrated in any model in this project; whether H2's binocularity is search or non-search binocular readout is not demonstrated; whether four blocks differ from six is not demonstrated. \section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The deployed model retains useful stereo prediction despite a candidate-invariant cost volume: all twelve slices identical by algebraic identity, 8.154\% D1 official-protocol accuracy, +89--90~pt binocular dependence under the labelled EXP-007 protocol, the frozen volume still consuming $L_f - R_f$ at one alignment. Candidate-varying cost-volume geometry contributes materially to the trained solution under this recipe, interacting with readout conditioning. Each standard correspondence signature admits an explicit correspondence-free counterexample, with two structural obstructions, so genuine local geometric correspondence is not demonstrated---which is not the same as showing it absent. Stereo performance and identifiable geometric correspondence can come apart, and mechanism claims need stronger evidence than the standard signatures.
